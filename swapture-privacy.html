Privacy Policy: Swapture

MMU ByteWorks · Effective 1 November 2026

Swapture ("the app") is a picture puzzle game made by MMU ByteWorks ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: your game data and your photos stay on your phone; the only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads.

Information that stays on your device

  • Game progress and settings: scores, best results, streaks, level, sound and theme choices, and the optional player name you type. These are stored only in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • Your photos: when you choose "Your photo", you pick a picture with the Android photo picker. The app makes a small square copy (at most 1024 px) in its private storage so you can play with it. The original photo and its location in your gallery are never stored. Copies are never uploaded, shared or logged. The app keeps your 12 most recent copies, and you can delete them all at any time in Settings → Delete my photos. The app asks for no storage or camera permission.
  • Backup: if Android backup is on, your phone may back up the game progress and settings (not your photos) to your own Google account, under Google's terms. You control this in your phone's settings.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads appear on menus and results screens, never while you are solving a puzzle. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location);
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes of the ad SDK;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Some features let you choose to watch a rewarded ad (for example, to double the XP from a round or to get a hint). Watching is always optional.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts or servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information ourselves.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services other than what the Google Mobile Ads SDK does to show ads.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Use Settings → Reset progress and Settings → Delete my photos, or uninstall the app, to remove it. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
